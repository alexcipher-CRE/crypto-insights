🔒 Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

What Data We Collect

Website Analytics

We use Plausible Analytics (self-hosted, privacy-focused) to understand how visitors use our site. Plausible does NOT use cookies and does NOT collect personal data. The data collected includes:

  • Page views and referral sources
  • Country-level location (from IP, not stored)
  • Device/browser type

No personal data is stored. No cookies are set. No tracking scripts are used.

Google AdSense

We display ads from Google AdSense. Google may use cookies to serve ads based on your usage. Google's advertising partner may place cookies on your browser to collect information about your interests across the web. This is standard in the advertising industry.

You can disable personalized ads by visiting Google Ad Settings or by visiting optout.aboutads.info.

Log Data

Like most websites, our server (GitHub Pages) automatically collects log data when you visit. This includes:

  • Your IP address (not stored, only used for abuse prevention)
  • Browser type and version
  • Time and date of your visit
  • The pages you visit

This data is automatically deleted and not used for identification purposes.

Cookies

We do not use cookies for tracking purposes. Any cookies set by third-party ad networks (Google AdSense) are governed by their own privacy policies.

Your Rights

  • You have the right to know what data we collect
  • You have the right to object to the processing of your data
  • You have the right to data portability
  • You have the right to request deletion of your data

To exercise these rights, contact us at: Open a GitHub issue.

Third-Party Services

We may display links to third-party websites or services that are not operated by us. If you click on a third-party link, you will be directed to that third party's site. We strongly advise you to review the Privacy Policy of every site you visit.

Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. Changes are effective immediately upon posting.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us by opening an issue on our GitHub repository.
